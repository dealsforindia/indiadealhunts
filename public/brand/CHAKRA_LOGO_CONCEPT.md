# IndiaDealHunts Tiranga Chakra identity

The user requested a Tiranga 3D logo with a Chakra and without a lightning mark. The existing blue and Tiranga lightning assets remain intact.

## Delivered assets

- `indiadealhunts-chakra.svg`: editable vector, exact 24-spoke navy Chakra, three horizontal Tiranga bands, silver rim and shallow illustrated depth. Used in the navbar and footer. 128 × 128 viewBox, rendered at 44 CSS pixels. Decorative image; the adjacent wordmark names the brand.
- `indiadealhunts-chakra-3d-v2.png`: transparent generated 3D concept. Used in the interactive logo preview, where pointer tilt is disabled for reduced motion and touch input.
- `/logo-preview.html`: light/dark preview with download link. No WebGL or Three.js runtime is required.

## Full image generation prompt

Create one premium 3D brand icon for IndiaDealHunts, an Indian shopping discovery brand. Transparent background. NO text, NO letters, NO lightning bolt, NO price symbol. A compact elegant circular medallion with a gently beveled polished silver outer rim and shallow visible silver extrusion, viewed almost straight-on with a subtle 10 degree perspective. The face is divided into three crisp horizontal equal Tiranga bands: rich saffron orange on top, pristine pearl white middle, rich Indian green bottom. A perfectly legible navy blue Ashoka Chakra with 24 thin spokes sits centered on the white band, large enough to read but fully contained in the white band. Tasteful realistic studio lighting, tactile glossy enamel face, restrained reflections, no excessive glow, very crisp silhouette, meticulously clean symmetry. The Chakra is the hero of the design. Design for use as a small website logo; fill about 80 percent of the square canvas. No decorative background, no plinth, no floating extra objects, no accompanying wordmark. High end product rendering, timeless sophisticated brand mark.

The raster rendering is a visual concept. The navbar vector has deterministic 24-spoke geometry and does not depend on image-generation precision.
